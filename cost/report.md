## Formulas

`item_cost = billed_units × price_per_unit` for mutually exclusive items (uncached input, 5-minute cache write, cache read, output; output already includes any thinking tokens, none here because thinking is disabled). `total = Σ item_cost`. Every charged line is in `cost/usage.csv`, mapped to its `request_id` in `cost/pilot_calls.jsonl`.
